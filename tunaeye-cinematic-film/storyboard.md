## Authenticity

The supplied photos are the actual `public/DEMO_SAMPLES` PNGs; the logo and Open Runde font are repository assets. The film introduces no people, physical kiosk footage, automated weighing, automatic market pricing, measured freshness, Grad-CAM, combined grade fusion, researcher role or direct ESC/POS claims.

The real Camera Capture button fetches a deterministic `/snapshot` fixture in a disposable Playwright browser. Separate `/grade` responses supply Grade A /96.3% for sashibo and Grade A /94.1% for tail. Eli overrides tail to B with a recorded reason; the original A remains. Two individual records persist locally and pending. The native UI displays its configured peso estimates and same-fish average confidence; commercial copy does not make an automatic pricing or grade-fusion promise.

MobileNetV3 is the user-supplied system architecture. This frontend checkout contains no model artifact that independently confirms it. The network is an editorial architecture diagram, not an inference trace. Physical hardware and live cloud services are outside the verification performed here.
